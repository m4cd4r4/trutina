Case Studies
Detailed analysis breakdowns for each demo case
Case 1: Sarah Mitchell — Legitimate Application
Score 12 · Approve
Clean application that passes all checks. Payslip produced by Xero, ABN active and name-matched, math consistent, bank credits align with payslip. Only minor flags: salary slightly above 75th percentile for the occupation, and standard Xero dual-font usage. Demonstrates that Trutina doesn't generate false positives on legitimate documents.
Case 2: James Chen — AI-Generated Payslip
Score 82 · Reject
Caught by multiple modules simultaneously. PDF created in Chrome (not a payroll platform), 5 different fonts (copy-paste assembly), gross minus tax doesn't equal net pay ($300 discrepancy), employer ABN registered to a completely different business, super rate at 7.5% instead of 11.5% SGC, and Claude detects AI-generation patterns including UK payroll terminology. Six flags, three critical.
Case 3: Emma Thompson — Cancelled ABN
Score 56 · Manual Review
The payslip looks professional (produced by MYOB), but cross-reference checks reveal the employer ABN was cancelled in October 2024 — 15 months before the payslip date. Employment letter references a different ABN than the payslip, and the claimed employment start date is after the ABN cancellation. Document was also modified 2 days before submission.
Case 4: David Kowalski — Forged Bank Statement
Score 68 · Manual Review
Bank statement closing balance inflated by $16,000 — opening + credits − debits = $42,318 but statement shows $58,318. Salary credit doesn't match payslip net income ($607 discrepancy). Submitted by Premier Lending Solutions, the same broker from the James Chen rejection — now flagged with a 30.4% fraud rate. Inconsistent page DPI suggests the closing balance page was separately produced.
Case 5: Priya Sharma — Broker Network Pattern
Score 45 · Manual Review
Individual payslip passes most checks (produced by KeyPay, math correct, ABN active). But Trutina's network analysis reveals this is the 4th application in 7 days from Fast Track Finance where the applicant claims employment at the same company — Pacific Coast Staffing, registered only 3 months ago. Four unrelated applicants, same small employer, same broker = statistically anomalous. Demonstrates broker behavioural profiling.
